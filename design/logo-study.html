01 · Construction

The C is a wrench head holding a nut.

The letter C is the round head of an open-end wrench. Its jaw is a half hexagon that grips a hex nut. The head is drawn on the cap height of Orbitron, so it sits in the word like a letter and still reads as a tool.

Head circleR = 50 units. Cap height = 100 units, so the head spans baseline to cap line. Width scale .
JawParallel flats at ±, V back from a regular hexagon, depth , tilt °.
NutA regular hexagon at of the jaw, gap , tone .
Type at weight . The tines of the C match the stem weight of the letters.
02 · Mark family

One shape, four uses.

The wordmark is the primary mark. The icon adds a handle to the same head for places where the word does not fit: app icons, favicons, social avatars.

03 · Scale

Holds from billboard to favicon.

Below about 14 px, the nut fills in and the C reads as a solid disc with a notch. Use the icon, not the wordmark, under 16 px.

04 · Color

Safety yellow on black, first.

The mark is one color. The nut is the same color at lower opacity, so it stays one-color in print and can be cut from vinyl or metal as a separate part.

05 · Clear space

Give it one nut of air.

Keep a clear space around the wordmark equal to the across-flats size of the nut. Nothing else enters the dashed box.

clear space = nut size
06 · Misuse

Do not.

The C only works because it is drawn to the letters around it. Any change to the word, the font, or the proportions breaks that.
